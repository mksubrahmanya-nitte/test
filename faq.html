JG University is a Technology-Driven University with a strong industry tie-up enabling us to design programmes that evolve as per the future industry demands and thus enabling students to get into employment or self-employment seamlessly. Our faculty includes both academic and industry experts. JG University is UGC approved state-private university, established under the Gujarat Private University Act, 2009, sponsored by the ASIA Charitable Trust (ACT). ACT has been in the field of education since 1965, successfully managing 17 colleges and 3 schools.

JG University’s main campus is located at Asia Campus on Drive-In Road in Ahmedabad. It is situated in the center of the city and has quick access to the metro and other local transportation. We also have our city campus located at Navrangpura, Ahmedabad.

Students are admitted twice every year. The online application process for the July semester starts in February and the online application process for the January semester starts in September.

The University will administer its own JG University PhD entrance test(JGET) followed by Group Discussion and Viva for admission to doctoral programme.

Following parameters are considered for selection to a Ph.D programme at JG University:

  • Score in the JG University Entrance Test(JGET)
  • Overall academic achievement
  • The statement of purpose presented by the candidate
  • The potential of the purported research to contribute to existing body of knowledge

The candidates who have cleared NET/SLET or possess a valid MPhil degree and whose admission at MPhil level has been through an entrance test will be exempted from the entrance test. However, the final admission rests on their performance in Group Discussion and Viva.

A candidate is assigned a Guide by the University after admission to the PhD programme.
